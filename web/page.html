Edit trails

Click a trail to select it. Ctrl/Shift-click adds or removes trails; Shift-drag draws a box. Then set values below and apply. Changes are written to annotations/annotations.json, which is published.

Sources and accuracy

278 rail-trail source records from Trailspotting, supplemented by MassGIS, NHDOT, VTrans, RIDOT/RIGIS and CT DEEP. Downloaded October 8, 2026. Coverage varies; listings do not guarantee current access or cycling permission.

Approximate GPS matching: within 20 metres, similar direction, at least 100 continuous metres. Parallel roads may cause false matches. Repeated rides do not add to covered trail length. Unknown surface does not mean paved.

Some Rhode Island project statuses are old. Check the source before riding. Active railways and No Access layers are excluded.

GPS and notes stay on this computer. OpenStreetMap supplies the background for the visible area. Catalogs © their respective authors. Trailspotting is an attributed personal local copy.
